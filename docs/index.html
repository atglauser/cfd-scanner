    Method and constraints
    • Data: Yahoo Finance futures, index, spot and FX quotes, scanned once a day on weekdays. These are not a broker CFD feed: CFD prices can be offset from them (oil especially), so align levels with your own ticket before placing an order.
    • Trend: EMA 20/50/200 stack on 1D, 4H and 1H, weighted 2 / 1.5 / 1. Bias LONG or SHORT needs a weighted score of at least 0.5.
    • Setup: limit entry at the 4H EMA20 (averaged with a nearby swing level when they agree), stop 1 × ATR(4H) beyond entry or beyond the next swing level, target at the next opposite 4H swing level.
    • Sizing: risk = (stop distance + spread) × units + financing for the expected holding days. Units round down to the broker step. If the minimum size alone exceeds the budget, the setup is not tradable.
    • Constraints: max risk per trade (default 2%); max total open risk across the top setups (default 4%); same-currency FX positions count as one trade; skip setups whose spread + financing exceed 10% of the reward; prefer net R:R ≥ 1.5; flag any position whose margin exceeds 50% of the account.
    • Contract specs (spread, minimum size, step, margin, financing) are generic estimates for a retail CFD broker. Check yours: they change which setups are tradable.
    • Not checked: the economic calendar (central banks, CPI, inventories), weekend gaps, liquidity.
    Educational and decision-support tool. Not investment advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument. CFDs are leveraged products and most retail accounts lose money trading them. Raw data: latest.json.
